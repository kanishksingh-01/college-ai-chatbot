4. Entity Review & Justification (3NF Schema Analysis)
✔ Faculty Action Item: Formal Entity Review & Relational Justification
Entity Review & Justification Manifesto (3NF Schema Architecture)
Comprehensive analysis of the 4 core entities, structure, attributes, normalization, and relational flow as requested in faculty action items.
Entity Name Primary Key Foreign Key(s) Key Attributes System Role & Justification
Student roll_no None (Base Entity) name, course, cgpa, attendance Represents enrolled students. Enables personalized query responses regarding attendance percentage, detention risk, and academic standing.
Admin_User username None (Independent) password_hash, role Stores administrative credentials with salted SHA-256 hashes. Restricts knowledgebase CRUD operations and audit log exports to authorized staff.
College_Info id None category, keywords, question, answer The institutional knowledge base. Contains categorized FAQ entries (Admissions, Fees, Exams, Pune Campus Location, Detention Rubrics) matched by the NLP engine.
Chat_History id roll_no (FK) user_query, bot_response, timestamp Audit telemetry and analytics log. Records every student interaction, resolved FAQ answer, timestamp, and feedback for administrative review and training.
Relational Cardinality & Integrity Rules:
  • Student (1) — Submits — (N) Chat_History: A registered student can submit multiple inquiries over time. If a query is submitted anonymously by a guest, roll_no is set to NULL (optional 1:N).
  • College_Info (1) — Matched In — (N) Chat_History: A specific FAQ record can match multiple historical student questions across sessions.
  • Admin_User (1) — Manages — (N) College_Info: Authorized department administrators create and update knowledgebase records.
Why Exactly These 4 Entities? (Normalization Defense)
  • Eliminates Redundancy: Separating student personal data from chatbot logs prevents duplicating student names/CGPA in every chat record.
  • Third Normal Form (3NF): Every non-key attribute depends strictly on the primary key, the whole key, and nothing but the key.
  • Security Isolation: Administrator credentials remain physically and logically isolated from public FAQ and student interaction data.

5. Implementation of Student Detention Policy
✔ Faculty Action Item: Official Detention Policy Integration & Rubrics
Official Student Detention Policy & Chatbot Logic Implementation
Trained into Chatbot NLP Knowledge Base and Personalized Response Logic as clarified in Faculty Meeting.
1. THEORY DETENTION RUBRICS (Attendance Excluding Medical Grounds)
S.No Attendance Bracket Academic Rubrics & Requirements Eligibility Status
— Above 75.0% Regular academic standing. No additional rubrics required. ✔ Fully Eligible
1 60.0% to 75.0% End Sem Examination Paper Sets of previous 2 years to be solved in Open Book mode in the College. Eligible after solving sets
2 50.0% to 59.99% (1) End Sem Examination Paper Sets of previous 2 years solved in Open Book mode in College.
(2) Must appear for a prelim examination and score a minimum of 60% marks to get out of detention.		 Eligible if prelim ≥ 60%
3 Below 50.0% No consideration. Strictly detained from university end-semester examinations. ✘ Strictly Detained
2. PRACTICAL DETENTION RUBRICS (Note: Not more than 2 practicals to be conducted in a day)
S.No Attendance Bracket Practical Rubrics & Penalties Practical Eligibility
1 60.0% to 75.0% The student will not be eligible for the attendance mark component of internal practical marks. Attendance marks forfeited
2 50.0% to 59.99% Student must appear for a practical-based prelim examination and score a minimum of 60% marks to be eligible to get out of detention. Eligible if practical ≥ 60%
3 Below 50.0% No consideration. Strictly detained from practical examinations. ✘ Strictly Detained
Chatbot Engine Integration Details:
When a logged-in student asks "What is my attendance?", app.py evaluates their percentage against these exact 4 thresholds and returns their specific status, eligible exam status, and designated academic rubrics. Pune Campus Address (Wagholi, Pune - 412207) is active in all institutional FAQs.
